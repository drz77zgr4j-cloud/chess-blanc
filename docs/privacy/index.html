Privacy policy

Chess Blanc keeps your chess on your iPhone or iPad. There is no Chess Blanc account, no tracking and no advertising.

What stays on your device

Your opening progress, settings, imported games, game reviews and training history are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. We have no server for Chess Blanc and never receive this information. Deleting the app deletes it.

chess.com and Lichess

If you link an account, the app sends the username you type to chess.com's public API and/or Lichess's public API to download your public games and ratings, and, for chess.com, your public profile name and picture (the picture comes from chess.com's image server). Those requests go straight from your device to those services, which, like any website, can see your device's IP address; we do not see them. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests. The app keeps no cookies from them. You can unlink either account at any time on the You page, which removes that account's games from your device.

Chess Blanc is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Chess.com or Lichess.

Purchases

Chess Blanc Pro is sold and billed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple tells the app whether you have an active subscription.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs, and no advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location or microphone.

Children

Chess Blanc is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone.

Your rights

The app sends us nothing, so we hold no personal data from it. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and delete them when they are no longer needed. You can ask us to access or delete that correspondence at any time. If you have a question or a complaint, email us; in the UK you can also contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
