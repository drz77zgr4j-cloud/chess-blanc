Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: email hello@blancnotes.app. A real person reads every message.

Linking your games

Open You (tap your picture on Home) › Your games. Type your chess.com or Lichess username: no password, nothing is posted. Games sync each time you open the app; pull down on Games to sync now.

Chess Blanc Pro

The app is free with daily limits on new opening lines, Blunder check and positions from your games. Pro (monthly, with a free week for new subscribers) removes the limits and unlocks every board and piece set.

  • Restore a purchase: You › See Pro › Restore purchase.
  • Cancel: iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Chess Blanc Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.
  • Refunds: handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Game reviews

Reviews run on your iPhone with Stockfish, so they work offline and nothing leaves your phone. A review takes about 20 seconds per game.
